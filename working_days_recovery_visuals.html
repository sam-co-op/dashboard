EXECUTIVE INSIGHTS | Section 1: Working Day Pacing & The 1st Saturday Off Dynamic

  • The 1st Saturday Off Policy Explained: In debt collection operations, the 1st Saturday of the month is a declared floor rest day (March 7, April 4, May 2, June 6, July 4, August 1). All subsequent Saturdays are active calling days. This causes early buckets to span 6 to 8 calendar days (e.g. Days 1–8 in May due to Maharashtra Day + 1st Sat + Sunday), giving borrowers a weekend self-pay window before calling resumption.
  • WD 21–25 is the Universal Peak: Across all 6 months, WD 21–25 generates the single largest cash volume (averaging ₹35.8 Lakhs or 31.7% of monthly revenue, ₹2.15 Crores total across 1,219 unique accounts). July hits ₹43.71L and April hits ₹40.32L in this 5-day closing window.
  • Early Token vs Late Settlement Transition: WD 01–05 captures 692 accounts at ₹11,874 average ticket (routine borrower salary clearing), while WD 21–25 captures 1,219 accounts at ₹17,621 average ticket (+48.4% expansion), reflecting structured high-balance settlement wires.

If risk teams dynamically front-loaded moderate waiver slabs into WD 11-15, operations could flatten the month-end curve, reducing final week slippage risk and capturing borrower liquidity before it is diverted to competing debt collectors.
